Calculation Sources

Recruiter cost (status quo): Each recruiter's current payout uses marginal slab rates — like income tax brackets. SF and non-SF placements are counted separately, each climbing its own slab ladder. Enterprise brackets are 1–35 / 36–70 / 71–150 PRs; Corporate brackets are 1–25 / 26–50 / 51–150 PRs. Rates range from ₹130–300 per PR depending on SF status and slab. Recruiters below their vertical's target earn ₹0.

Recruiter cost (new scheme): Every eligible recruiter earns a flat-per-placement bonus (x) plus a retention bonus (y × months the worker stays, capped at 12). No SF/non-SF distinction. In "Flat" schemes, x is the same for everyone; in "Slab" schemes, x increases with the recruiter's slab tier. The attrition slider increases tenure, which increases both the y×T cost and the markup revenue.

TL & Manager cost (status quo): TLs and Managers earn per-PR rates based on their team's average productivity (total team PRs ÷ headcount). Eligibility requires average productivity to meet the vertical's TL/Manager target. Rates are ₹150/200/250 for TLs and ₹200/250/300 for Managers, with a cap of 100 attributed PRs per person. Current totals: ₹55,100 (TLs) + ₹85,950 (Managers).

TL & Manager cost (new scheme): Same x + y×T structure. TL base rates (x) match recruiter rates; Manager x = recruiter x + ₹50 (reflecting the current ₹50 premium). Slab assignment uses average team productivity. The y component uses each team's average tenure (improved by the attrition slider). PR cap of 100 still applies.

Revenue per recruiter: Each recruiter's revenue = (average monthly markup per placement × tenure in months × number of placements) + total sourcing fees. Markup and sourcing fees are client-specific, computed from each recruiter's actual client mix. The attrition slider increases tenure, which increases markup revenue proportionally.

Cross-sourcing: Cross-sourced placements carry a z cost (= z-factor × placer's x) on every cross-sourced PR. "Net new" placements additionally generate revenue (markup × tenure + sourcing fee per PR) and incur placer cost (x + y×T). "Substituted" placements only add the z cost — they don't change total PR count or revenue.

Δ Margin = (New scheme revenue − New scheme total cost) − (Status quo revenue − Status quo total cost). A positive delta means the new scheme produces higher net margins.
